# Engineering Maturity Model

## Aim

This lean maturity model tackles 5 aspects; Architecture and design, Development and quality, Delivery and change, Operations and observability, and Security and resilience, across 3 verticals: people, process and technology. The model is aimed to be a self assesment carried out by teams and based on CMMI, using their existing maturity levels.
